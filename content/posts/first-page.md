## The first page

A small place for things worth keeping: a question from a lesson, a useful piece of code, or a detail from an ordinary day.

A note can be short. A clear example, a few steps, and a link to the source are often enough to make it useful later. Questions can stay open until there is something more to add.

The writing and math examples here show a few ways to arrange those ideas, from a checklist to a worked calculation. Start with the part that matters, then return to it when a better explanation comes along.
